# RiskPulse AI

## Adaptive Risk-Based Pricing & Underwriting Intelligence Engine

RiskPulse AI is a proof-of-concept solution for P&C insurance that helps insurers evaluate changing risk, support adaptive pricing decisions, identify potential rating leakage, and assist underwriters with explainable risk insights.

## Problem

Traditional actuarial pricing cycles can be slow and reactive, which can lead to rating lag, adverse selection, rating leakage, and loss ratio pressure.

## Objective

The objective of RiskPulse AI is to provide an adaptive risk intelligence and underwriting decision-support solution that helps insurers:

- Evaluate changing customer risk
- Generate risk scores
- Recommend risk-based premiums
- Identify potential underpricing
- Provide explainable underwriting insights
- Support human underwriters in decision-making

## Key Capabilities

- Risk Assessment
- Adaptive Pricing Recommendation
- Rating Leakage Detection
- Explainable Risk Insights
- AI-Assisted Underwriting
- What-if Risk Simulation
- Risk Trend Monitoring

## Project Type

Proof of Concept (PoC)
